Privacy Notice

// Last updated July 2026

What this site collects

The only personal data this site collects is what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, and message. Nothing is collected just from browsing the site — there's no analytics or ad tracking here.

Where it goes

  • Web3Forms — processes the contact form and delivers your message to my inbox. It's a third-party service; it sees the data you submit in order to route it to me.
  • Cloudflare — sits in front of this site for DNS, TLS, and DDoS protection. It may set functional/security cookies as part of serving the page, independent of anything you submit.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the static files that make up this site.

What I do with it

I use whatever you submit only to reply to you. I don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or add you to any mailing list. If you'd rather not use the form, feel free to reach out through GitHub instead.
