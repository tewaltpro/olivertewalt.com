CyberEthos

CyberEthos is a lightweight digital accountability tool that encourages ethical and intentional technology use. Rather than monitoring users, it serves as a quiet reminder that every action online carries real-world responsibility.

About the Project

CyberEthos is designed to promote mindful decision-making while using a computer. Through gentle prompts and optional accountability features, it helps users pause, reflect, and make choices that align with their values.

Reflection Prompts

Brief reminders before uploads and downloads encourage intentional, responsible online behavior without interrupting normal workflows.

Panic Mode

Instantly disconnect network access and close selected applications when a moment of accountability is needed.

Privacy First

No accounts. No cloud storage. No telemetry. Everything stays on your own computer.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

CyberEthos does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to any server. All app settings (hotkey configuration, feature toggles, and local usage counters) are stored only on your own device in a local settings file and are never sent anywhere.

Network and System Access

CyberEthos can optionally disconnect network adapters and close specific applications when the Panic Mode feature is triggered. These actions are performed locally on your device and are not logged, stored, or transmitted.

Browser Extension

The CyberEthos browser extension observes file uploads and downloads only to display a brief reflection prompt before they proceed. It does not log, store, transmit, or analyze the files or their contents.

Third-Party Links

The app's Learn and Support sections link to external websites (such as staysafeonline.org, connectsafely.org, and cybercivilrights.org). These sites maintain their own independent privacy practices, which CyberEthos does not control.
